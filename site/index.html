Redpen

Mark up screenshots like a teacher grades a page.

Circle anything, say what's wrong, and it's written beside the circle in red pen. Then paste the set into your chat.

Get Redpen How it works

Free for macOS 15 or later.

Circle it

Circle anything. Say why.

Your scribble becomes a clean red circle, and Redpen starts listening. What you say is written right beside it. Click anywhere to leave a note there instead.

Talk it through

Long notes go under the image.

Talk as long as you like. Long notes move below the screenshot, numbered to match the mark, so a model reads them as plain text.

Screenshots

Take a screenshot. Redpen asks.

New screenshots put a red dot in the menu bar, and one notification asks after a burst. Or pick images from Photos.

Superwhisper is listening

Voice

Built for Superwhisper.

Circle something and Redpen starts Superwhisper for you. The transcript lands beside your mark. No Superwhisper? Mac dictation works too, on your Mac.
